Quick Start (3 Steps)

  1. Enter Bootloader Mode: Hold the B (BOOT) button on your XIAO ESP32-S3, plug it into USB-C (or tap R), then release B.
    Already running v1.5.0+? Hold Assistant + Capture + Y (2 s) or click Enter USB Bootloader at http://192.168.4.1 — no button press needed.
  2. Flash: Click Connect & Install Firmware above, choose the USB JTAG/serial debug unit port, and wait for flashing to complete. Unplug and replug the dongle.
  3. Auto-Pair Your Controller: Put any Wi-Fi Stadia Controller into orange-blink setup mode near the dongle. The ESP32-S3 automatically pairs it over Bluetooth LE within seconds — no phone app or PC tool required.
Prefer CLI flashing (esptool)? 
python3 -m esptool --chip esp32s3 --baud 460800 \
  --before default-reset --after hard-reset write-flash \
  --flash-mode dio --flash-freq 80m --flash-size 8MB \
  0x0 stadiacast-esp32-merged.bin
Updating later over Wi-Fi (OTA) — no USB cable flashing needed

Once flashed with v1.5.0+, connect any phone or PC to the dongle's Wi-Fi (STADIA_ESP32 / stadiacast) and open http://192.168.4.1 to upload stadiacast-esp32.bin in your browser, configure settings, or save your Home Wi-Fi for 1-button controller updates (Assistant + Capture + B).
